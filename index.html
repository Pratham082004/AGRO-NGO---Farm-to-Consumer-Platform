How AgroNGO Works

Three simple steps to bridge the gap between farms and dining tables.

01

Farmers List Harvest

Farmers upload harvest details. Our Machine Learning models calculate optimal price recommendations and shelf-life estimates.

02

Buyers Discover Produce

Retail buyers, consumers, and food distributors browse transparent listings filtered by state, district, and crop type.

03

Direct Fulfillment

Orders are placed directly. Produce is packaged and dispatched via farmer delivery or local logistics with instant payment settlement.

Powered by AgroNGO ML Engine

Intelligent Machine Learning Predictions

We integrate trained Python Machine Learning models into the marketplace to protect perishable crops and empower farmers with data.

📅

Shelf Life & Expiry Prediction

Predicts how many days produce will stay fresh based on crop category, harvest date, and storage conditions (ambient, cold storage, refrigerated).

💡

Dynamic Price Recommendation

Recommends fair per-kg pricing to farmers depending on crop stock, state location, processing level, and seasonal market demand.

Why Choose AgroNGO?

Zero Middleman Cut

Farmers keep 100% of their earnings without commission deductions by APMC agents or intermediaries.

Guaranteed Freshness

Direct farm harvesting means produce arrives significantly fresher than traditional market supply chains.

SMS & Call Support

Farmers without smartphones can update crop listings and receive order alerts via SMS and call center support.

Flexible Fulfillment

Choose between farmer self-delivery, buyer pickup, or verified regional courier logistics.

Transparent Payments

Support for Cash on Delivery (COD) and Paytm UPI digital payments directly to farmer bank accounts.

Farmer Group Unions

Form regional farmer collectives to aggregate produce quantities and negotiate bulk buyer logistics.
